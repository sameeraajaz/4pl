# Website review and implementation

The first version established a coherent palette and illustration system, but its service descriptions were thin. Most service detail was confined to dialogs, the distinction between freight execution and fourth-party logistics was under-explained, and the website lacked dedicated search entry points.

| Area | Earlier version | Updated implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand and hierarchy | Repeated general slogans | Clear freight/4PL positioning, stronger hero, consistent contrast and alternating content sections |
| Conversion | Generic quote trigger | Visible route planner, mode-specific service calls to action and a fuller shipment brief |
| Services | Four short cards and dialogs | Four linked HTML service pages with distinct cargo, transport, scope and enquiry content |
| 4PL explanation | Brief overview | Clear operating-model section, 3PL vs 4PL comparison and a dedicated explanatory guide |
| Customer questions | No FAQ section | Relevant homepage and service-specific questions with native expandable answers |
| Useful content | No supporting guides | Original 3PL vs 4PL guide and freight shipping checklist with a downloadable text version |
| Technical SEO | Minimal homepage metadata | Unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, structured data, sitemap, robots and social sharing metadata |
| Page assets | External font dependency | Locally bundled fonts and SVG assets with dimensions and font licensing |
| Trust | No verified company details | No fabricated proof; verified company footprint and contact data remain a launch dependency |

The useful patterns were clear service navigation, prominent shipment actions, explanations of the logistics model and relevant supporting information. The new copy and illustration system are original; competitor statistics, partnerships, service promises and credentials have not been transferred to 4PL.

## Search intent and page mapping
| Page | Main intent |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Freight forwarding and integrated logistics overview |
| Ocean freight | Sea freight, FCL/LCL and container shipment planning |
| Air freight | Time-sensitive air cargo, chargeable weight and shipment requirements |
| Road transport | FTL/LTL, inland connections and delivery conditions |
| 4PL supply chain management | Fourth-party logistics and provider coordination |
| 3PL vs 4PL guide | Understanding the operating-model difference |
| Freight shipping checklist | Preparing complete freight enquiries |
| About | Understanding the company's proposed approach |
| Contact | Preparing shipment requirements |

This is a general logistics content strategy. No keyword-volume estimates, backlink assessment or competitive ranking difficulty study was performed. Local SEO requires confirmed operating locations, service coverage and contact details. Implementation improves content clarity and crawlability; it does not guarantee indexing, rankings or enquiries.
