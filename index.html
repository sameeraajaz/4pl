Understand the options before you plan the shipment.

What is fourth-party logistics (4PL)?
Fourth-party logistics coordinates providers, transport and processes across a supply chain. Instead of managing each activity separately, the focus is on the operating plan, handoffs and information connecting the whole journey. Explore 4PL supply chain management.
How is 4PL different from 3PL?
A 3PL typically handles logistics execution, such as transport, storage or fulfilment. A 4PL coordinates a broader network of providers and activities. The exact responsibilities depend on the agreed service scope. Read the 3PL vs 4PL guide.
Should I choose ocean, air or road freight?
Start with the route, cargo size, ready date and delivery deadline. Ocean freight often suits larger international volumes; air freight can support time-sensitive cargo; road transport connects inland locations and the first and final legs. Availability and suitability must be checked for your shipment.
What information is needed to plan a shipment?
Provide the origin, destination, cargo description, packed dimensions, number of pieces, gross weight and desired timing. Include special handling needs and the collection or delivery conditions. Use the freight shipping checklist.
Can transport modes be combined in one plan?
A shipment may combine ocean, air and road legs. A coordinated plan defines the handoffs, responsibilities and schedule between them. The available combinations depend on your cargo and route.
Can I get a live quote or track a shipment here?
This version creates a downloadable shipment brief and offers a clearly labelled tracking demo. It does not submit enquiries, calculate prices or connect to carrier systems. Live services need to be connected before launch.
