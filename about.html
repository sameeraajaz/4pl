The way we think about logistics

Look beyond
the next shipment.

Every movement connects to something bigger: your operation, your customers and the next decision. That is where a 4PL approach begins.

Logistics works better as a connected operation.

A shipment may pass through several providers, locations and internal teams. Each can perform its own task well while gaps remain between them. A fourth-party logistics approach considers those connections as part of the service plan.

4PL brings a supply chain perspective to freight forwarding and logistics planning. Start with the business requirement, consider the transport options and define the responsibilities and information needed across the journey.

Four principles behind a clearer plan

Understand first.

Begin with the cargo, route and operational priorities before deciding on a solution.

Define the handoffs.

Make responsibilities and information requirements clear between each stage.

Consider the whole journey.

Connect first-mile, main transport and final delivery requirements.

Keep improving.

Review the operating plan as volumes, routes and business needs change.

Start at the right scale.

A single consignment may need a focused freight solution. A recurring operation with multiple providers may call for wider coordination. Explore the difference between 3PL and 4PL, or begin with your shipment details.
