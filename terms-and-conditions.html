1. About these website terms

These Terms & Conditions describe use of the 4PL website, its information and its shipment-planning tools. They apply to the website experience, rather than establishing a transport, storage, customs, insurance or freight-forwarding contract.

This version is a website preview. The operator’s registered legal name, registered address and contact details have not yet been published. This draft must be completed and approved before the website is used to accept commercial instructions.

2. Information and service descriptions

Website content provides general information about logistics solutions. A service description does not establish that a particular route, cargo type, capacity, deadline or handling arrangement is available. Confirm requirements and availability for the specific shipment.

Educational guides explain general concepts. They do not replace shipment-specific operational, customs, tax, insurance or legal advice.

3. Shipment tools and downloadable briefs

The homepage planner and shipment forms help you prepare a text brief. In the current version, entered details are handled in the page and a download link is generated locally. Creating or downloading a brief does not submit an enquiry, reserve space, produce a quotation or create a booking.

You decide whether to download the file and who receives it afterwards. Review the contents before sharing. Do not enter payment-card information, identity documents, account passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Quotations, bookings and service agreements

A future quotation or booking needs confirmed cargo details, the relevant contracting entity, the agreed scope and separate commercial acceptance. Charges, payment terms, transit estimates, carrier conditions, insurance arrangements and any applicable liabilities must be set out in the relevant agreement.

This website does not adopt a transport convention, FIATA trading terms, liability limit, jurisdiction or insurance policy on your behalf. Such provisions must be assessed for the actual service and agreed separately where applicable.

5. Demonstration tracking

The tracking tool displays an illustrative shipment only when the reference 4PL-DEMO-001 is used. The sample origin, destination and status are demonstration data. They do not represent live cargo movements, confirmed service coverage or a delivery commitment.

Do not rely on the demonstration to make collection, payment, production or delivery decisions.

6. Responsible website use

Use the website lawfully and provide information you are authorised to use. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, obtain unauthorised access, introduce malicious code, impersonate another person or use the tools to prepare misleading instructions.

Only include personal information relevant to a legitimate business enquiry. Obtain any necessary authority before including another person’s details.

7. Content and external links

Website text, illustrations, branding and other material may be protected by intellectual-property rights. You may use the planning tools and download the provided checklist or your own brief for legitimate business purposes. Other reproduction or commercial reuse requires permission from the relevant rights holder unless permitted by law.

External links are provided for reference. The linked organisation controls its own content, services and privacy practices. A link does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

8. Availability and responsibilities

The website may be changed, interrupted or unavailable. Check important information before relying on it, and retain your own copy of any brief you download. A browser-generated file is not evidence that 4PL received or accepted an instruction.

Nothing in these website terms removes rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Freight-service responsibilities and any permitted limitations require an appropriate service agreement and applicable-law review.

9. Privacy

The Privacy Policy explains the current handling of form entries, downloads, tracking-demo inputs and potential hosting records. If collection or integrations change, the policy and the notices next to the forms must be updated before those changes are introduced.

10. Updates, applicable law and contact

This draft was last updated on 15 November 2026. Changes to website terms will be identified through the updated version date. Separate service agreements are not amended merely by changing a website page.

No governing-law choice or dispute forum is specified in this preview.
